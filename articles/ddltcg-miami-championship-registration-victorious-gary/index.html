DDLTCG Championship Registration Is Open: How Victorious Gary Gets You Into Miami

By ReefCommunity Builder · Nov 18, 2026 · 4 min read

If you have been seeing golden armored Gary on a throne all over your timeline this week, here is why. The first official Doginal Dogs Legends TCG Championship is getting real, and registration just opened. I put together a plain guide so nobody in the pack misses a step. Every fact below links back to a post or page you can check yourself.

What just happened

At 10 AM ET on Wednesday, November 18, Legends TCG posted that registration is officially open. The rule is simple: if you purchased a Victorious Gary promo card, you are eligible to take part in the first official DDL TCG Championship.

The window is short. The official post says registration will be open for 48 hours, and the graphic says "48 hours only." If you plan to play, do not leave this for the weekend.

The two steps

According to the official post, every participant must do two things:

1. Fill out the registration form linked in that post. 2. Join the Doginal Dogs Discord at discord.gg/doginaldogs.

After registration closes, Legends TCG says it will post a schedule. Only use the form link from the official @DDLTCG account. Fake links show up fast whenever there is a deadline.

What is Victorious Gary?

Victorious Gary is a promo card, which the official shop page describes as a special DDL card you cannot get in booster packs. The shop calls it super limited and says it is the only way to enter the DDL Championship.

The card art shows Gary in golden armor sitting on a throne. The image Legends TCG shared in its announcement thread labels it promo V112, a Neutral Creature, with the play text "Draw a card." That same post says every single participant will receive one exclusive Giga Rare Gary promo card.

Here are the key details from the shop page, in plain words:

  • Price: $99.
  • No purchase limit: you can buy as many cards as you want.
  • One entry per buyer: Championship entries are limited to one per buyer, no matter how many cards you buy.
  • The entry is a bonus: it has no cash value, cannot be transferred, and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Shipping: the card ships in December 2026.
  • All sales are final, except where the law requires otherwise or if an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered.

How we got here

Legends TCG first announced the Championship on October 24, the same weekend as the Charlotte pop-out. Then on November 10 it posted that registration was live, with the Victorious Gary shop link open until 12 AM ET on Monday, November 16.

The community pushed hard that week. @lurnmeta laid out the steps: grab the card, do it before the Monday deadline, and pull up on December 9 at Collectors Club during DD Miami. @BooMetaX posted a last call the night the window closed, @PawsMeta and @Saltymetax shared that they had locked theirs in, and @WelshBullTrader said he grabbed one to show support even though he cannot attend.

So the order was: buy the promo card first, then register as a player now. If you bought a Victorious Gary, today's form is your next step.

The event itself

The Championship is on December 9, from 11 AM to 3 PM, at the Collectors Club venue in Miami, during DD Miami, December 7 to 9. The game is the first set, Rise of the Pack, which is printed right on the official tournament pass graphic.

In October, Legends TCG described the path to Miami as a round robin online tournament in the week before the event, with the top 16 players moving on to the in-person finals. The exact schedule is still to come, so watch @DDLTCG after registration closes for the final details.

Quick checklist

  • Confirm you purchased a Victorious Gary promo card.
  • Fill out the form from the official post inside the 48 hour window.
  • Join discord.gg/doginaldogs.
  • Turn on notifications for @DDLTCG so you catch the schedule.
  • Get reps in. The online rounds come before Miami, so practice now.

I am not telling anyone to buy anything. This is just a guide so the people who already planned to play do not miss the window. Not financial advice. Do your own research.

Whether you are flying to Miami or cheering from home, this is a big moment for Legends. Lock in your spot, then come find me in the replies and tell me what deck you are bringing.

Quick answers

How do you enter the first official DDLTCG Championship?
Entry runs through the Victorious Gary promo card. If you purchased one, fill out the official registration form linked by @DDLTCG and join the Doginal Dogs Discord at discord.gg/doginaldogs during the 48 hour registration window that opened on November 18, 2026.
How much does the Victorious Gary promo card cost and when does it ship?
The official Doginal Dogs shop lists it at $99. There is no limit on how many cards you can buy, but Championship entries are limited to one per buyer, and the card ships in December 2026.
When and where is the DDLTCG Championship?
It is on December 9 at the Collectors Club venue in Miami, during DD Miami (December 7 to 9). Legends TCG says it will post a schedule after registration closes.
