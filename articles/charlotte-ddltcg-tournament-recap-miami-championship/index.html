From Charlotte to Miami: The NC DDLTCG Tournament Recap and How the Dec 9 Championship Works

By ReefCommunity Builder · Oct 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Last weekend, Doginal Dogs Legends left the screen and hit real tables in Charlotte, North Carolina. A lot of people asked me what happened and what comes next, so here is a recap of the NC tournament plus a plain guide to the first official Legends TCG Championship in Miami. Every fact below links back to a post you can check yourself.

The Charlotte pop-out

The event was billed as a Doginal Dogs pop-out and a Pre-Miami Tournament. Players gathered at Get Some Games in Charlotte on Saturday, October 24, and the game on the tables was the first set, Rise of the Pack.

Not everyone could be there, so the community brought it online. @MetalXMeta went live as games kicked off on Saturday afternoon and kept the stream going into the night. @BooMetaX and @Rockmetax posted from the tables, and @realmjmetax said the NC event was trending on X.

Some of my favorite posts were the small moments. @BiiigTechOG shared his first ever in-person match, a 2-0 loss to @NebMeta, and joked that neither of them had any full arts. That is what these events are about: people who have been playing online sitting across from each other for the first time.

The results

The tournament ran late, with best of 3 semifinals. @Andmetax took the NC title, and @BooMetaX, @NebMeta and @zackmetax made strong runs to the semifinals. Host @Rockmetax posted the champ in the early hours of Sunday, and congratulations from @realmjmetax, @SparKMetaX and many others followed.

And used his own post to thank @Rockmetax and @realmjmetax for hosting and pulling everything together, and to give a shout to everyone who showed up to play. Even outside the community, Dogecoin Daily wrote up how Bark spent the weekend resharing posts from the Carolina tournament.

Next stop: the Miami Championship

On the same day Charlotte kicked off, Legends TCG announced the first ever official DDLTCG Championship. Here are the key details from the official thread:

  • When and where: December 9, from 11 AM to 3 PM, at the Collectors Club venue in Miami. It falls during DD Miami, December 7 to 9.
  • How to qualify: everyone who registers is invited to a round robin online tournament in the week before the event. The top 16 players advance to the in-person finals, where one champion is crowned.
  • Prizes: first place gets an ultra rare Gary promo card, a rare Doginal Dog and 10 boxes of Rise of the Pack. Second place gets an ultra rare Gary promo card and 5 boxes. Third place gets 5 boxes. That comes straight from the official prize post.
  • How to sign up: use the registration form linked by @DDLTCG. Only use the link from the official account.

How to get reps in before December

The online qualifier means practice matters, and there are plenty of ways to play right now. Doginal Dogs Legends Brackets posted its schedule for October 26 to November 1. It includes a Legends For Good tournament and Legends KOTH on Monday, a daily Legends Academy show with Anthem and Allies at 3 PM ET, and a Howloween Haunt tournament on Saturday at 7:30 PM ET.

KOTH, short for King of the Hill, already has some history. On October 21, @Hofers became the new KOTH champion, beating @Lxpold 2-0 in the finals and then reigning champ @GambitMeta 2-1.

A few tips from someone who watched the whole weekend unfold:

  • Play best of 3. Charlotte's semifinals were best of 3, so practice adjusting between games, not just winning one.
  • Know your spells. Cards like Siphon, a 5-cost spell that destroys a Creature and restores 3 HP to your Hero if you control a Wizard, can swing a match.
  • Show up live. Watching streams and tournaments is the fastest way to learn what other players are building.

Why this matters

Ten thousand pixel dogs turned into a card game, and that card game now fills tables in North Carolina and is heading to a championship in Miami. Whether you play, collect, or just love the art, there is a seat at the table for you.

Congrats again to And, and thank you to everyone who hosted, streamed and played. See you in the brackets, and maybe in Miami.
