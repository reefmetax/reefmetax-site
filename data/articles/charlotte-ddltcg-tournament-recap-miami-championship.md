Last weekend, Doginal Dogs Legends left the screen and hit real tables in Charlotte, North Carolina. A lot of people asked me what happened and what comes next, so here is a recap of the NC tournament plus a plain guide to the first official Legends TCG Championship in Miami. Every fact below links back to a post you can check yourself.

## The Charlotte pop-out

The event was billed as a Doginal Dogs pop-out and a [Pre-Miami Tournament](https://x.com/Rockmetax/status/2113793089143751148). Players gathered at [Get Some Games in Charlotte](https://x.com/Rockmetax/status/2114111520325943346) on Saturday, October 24, and the game on the tables was the first set, Rise of the Pack.

Not everyone could be there, so the community brought it online. [@MetalXMeta went live](https://x.com/MetalXMeta/status/2114090639402889565) as games kicked off on Saturday afternoon and [kept the stream going](https://x.com/MetalXMeta/status/2114164983982616671) into the night. [@BooMetaX](https://x.com/BooMetaX/status/2114095457957785713) and [@Rockmetax](https://x.com/Rockmetax/status/2114125436007375209) posted from the tables, and [@realmjmetax said](https://x.com/realmjmetax/status/2114161495089012757) the NC event was trending on X.

Some of my favorite posts were the small moments. [@BiiigTechOG shared](https://x.com/BiiigTechOG/status/2114190234174759188) his first ever in-person match, a 2-0 loss to @NebMeta, and joked that neither of them had any full arts. That is what these events are about: people who have been playing online sitting across from each other for the first time.

## The results

The tournament ran late, with best of 3 semifinals. [@Andmetax took the NC title](https://x.com/Reefmetax/status/2114359253653688722), and @BooMetaX, @NebMeta and @zackmetax made strong runs to the semifinals. Host [@Rockmetax posted the champ](https://x.com/Rockmetax/status/2114238713009942763) in the early hours of Sunday, and congratulations from [@realmjmetax](https://x.com/realmjmetax/status/2114393719273054573), [@SparKMetaX](https://x.com/SparKMetaX/status/2114811763535552873) and many others followed.

And used his own post to [thank @Rockmetax and @realmjmetax](https://x.com/Andmetax/status/2114401027898196287) for hosting and pulling everything together, and to give a shout to everyone who showed up to play. Even outside the community, [Dogecoin Daily wrote up](https://x.com/dogecoindailydd/status/2114382602199347414) how Bark spent the weekend resharing posts from the Carolina tournament.

## Next stop: the Miami Championship

On the same day Charlotte kicked off, Legends TCG [announced the first ever official DDLTCG Championship](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028186531185082). Here are the key details from the official thread:

- **When and where:** [December 9, from 11 AM to 3 PM](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028202255331698), at the Collectors Club venue in Miami. It falls during [DD Miami, December 7 to 9](https://x.com/devinteerfilms/status/2112934361339175376).
- **How to qualify:** everyone who registers is invited to a [round robin online tournament](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028230365553028) in the week before the event. The top 16 players advance to the in-person finals, where one champion is crowned.
- **Prizes:** first place gets an ultra rare Gary promo card, a rare Doginal Dog and 10 boxes of Rise of the Pack. Second place gets an ultra rare Gary promo card and 5 boxes. Third place gets 5 boxes. That comes straight from the [official prize post](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028270463426624).
- **How to sign up:** use the [registration form linked by @DDLTCG](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028285533265954). Only use the link from the official account.

## How to get reps in before December

The online qualifier means practice matters, and there are plenty of ways to play right now. Doginal Dogs Legends Brackets posted its [schedule for October 26 to November 1](https://x.com/kennelDAO/status/2114766068530987050). It includes a Legends For Good tournament and Legends KOTH on Monday, a daily Legends Academy show with Anthem and Allies at 3 PM ET, and a Howloween Haunt tournament on Saturday at 7:30 PM ET.

KOTH, short for King of the Hill, already has some history. On October 21, [@Hofers became the new KOTH champion](https://x.com/kennelDAO/status/2113099141274554834), beating @Lxpold 2-0 in the finals and then reigning champ @GambitMeta 2-1.

A few tips from someone who watched the whole weekend unfold:

- **Play best of 3.** Charlotte's semifinals were best of 3, so practice adjusting between games, not just winning one.
- **Know your spells.** Cards like Siphon, a 5-cost spell that [destroys a Creature](https://x.com/realmjmetax/status/2114845526982115633) and restores 3 HP to your Hero if you control a Wizard, can swing a match.
- **Show up live.** Watching streams and tournaments is the fastest way to learn what other players are building.

## Why this matters

Ten thousand pixel dogs turned into a card game, and that card game now fills tables in North Carolina and is heading to a championship in Miami. Whether you play, collect, or just love the art, there is a seat at the table for you.

Congrats again to And, and thank you to everyone who hosted, streamed and played. See you in the brackets, and maybe in Miami.
